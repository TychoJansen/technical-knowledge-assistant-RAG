Generated by `rag eval answers`; do not edit by hand.

- **Prompt**: fingerprint of the assistant prompt; compare only runs with the same one.
- **Ctx Cov / Ctx R**: Coverage@k and Recall@k of the k chunks given to the model (retrieval ceiling).
- **Nugget R**: share of each question's nuggets the judge finds in the answer (0 when abstained).
- **Nugget R|ans**: the same, over answered questions only.
- **Answered / False abst / Invalid**: share of (answerable) FreshStack questions answered, wrongly abstained, or with unparseable output.
- **Cit support**: share of valid citations the judge finds supporting the answer, with a quote that is verified to occur in the source.
- **Cit prec**: share of citations that are relevant according to the FreshStack judgments (lower bound).
- **Uncited ans**: share of answers without any valid citation.
